The problem

Most stores don't have a lead problem. They have a system problem.

You already get interest. The gap is everything that happens after — and it's quietly costing you buyers every single month.

Leads slip through the cracks

Someone requests pricing, nobody follows up fast, and by next week they've bought from the store across town. No system means every slow reply is a lost sale.

Your website informs — it doesn't sell

Most hot tub sites read like a brochure. They list models and hours, but never guide a serious buyer toward booking a wet test or requesting a quote.

You go invisible off-season

Marketing runs when you remember to run it. Between buying seasons the pipeline goes quiet, and every spring you start cold instead of full.

Selectivity

We're not for every store. That's the point.

One partner per market only works if we choose carefully. Here's who we say yes to — and who we don't.

We're a fit if…

  • You own an established showroom and want to dominate your local market
  • You're doing real volume and ready to invest in growth, not the cheapest fix
  • You'll treat this as a partnership and actually work the leads we send
  • You want to be the obvious choice in your city — before a competitor claims it

We're not a fit if…

  • You're shopping purely on price and want the cheapest option available
  • You're brand new or pre-revenue and don't yet have a showroom
  • You want us to work with your direct competitor down the road, too
  • You're after a quick hack instead of a system you'll run for years
